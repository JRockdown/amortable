What Amortable computes

A time-value-of-money workbench in the spirit of professional amortization software. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

TVM Solver — the five classic variables N, i%, PV, PMT, FV. Fix any four, solve the fifth. Separate payment / compounding frequencies and begin- or end-of-period timing. Sign convention: money received is positive, money paid is negative.

Amortization — full period-by-period schedule with interest, principal, and running balance; handles balloons / lease residuals, extra principal, any payment frequency, and variable-rate steps: enter rate changes and the payment is re-amortized (ARM-style recast) over the remaining balance and term. Export to CSV.

Cash Flow · NPV · IRR — build an uneven stream from repeated events. Two timing bases: Periodic (evenly spaced) and Dated, which places every flow on a real calendar date and discounts with your choice of day-count convention — Actual/365, Actual/360, 30/360 (US), or Actual/Actual — giving true XNPV and XIRR. Mark any one amount Unknown and it's solved in closed form so the stream nets to zero at your rate — the classic "what payment makes this deal work?" question.

Also reported: MIRR, payback period, and the undiscounted net, with per-period present values tabulated.

Still on the roadmap: APR / Reg-Z disclosure output, points & fees, and skipped / irregular per-event dates. Ask and I'll add them.
